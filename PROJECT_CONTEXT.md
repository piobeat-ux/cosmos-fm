## 3. Strategic implication for Cosmos FM

Cosmos FM should be designed and positioned not only for the current 2026 network, but for a group expected to scale materially by 2030.

This means the business model, content operating model and product architecture should support:

- growth from **55 to 160+ hotels**;
- growth from **5,000+ to 25,000 employees**;
- distributed operation across **all federal districts**;
- a network where **80% of room inventory is expected to be franchise / asset-light**;
- multiple hotel brands and price segments within one media ecosystem.

For business decisions, this growth trajectory is part of the core rationale for making Cosmos FM scalable, centrally governed and locally extensible.
